ReciGuard

A personal recipe and dietary need manager.

ReciGuard is a recipe manager built for hosting. Save and organize your own recipes, then connect with friends using a friend code to keep track of their dietary needs and allergies — so you always know what's safe to cook for them.

Download on the App Store — coming soon

Features

  • Save recipes with ingredients, steps, and your own notes.
  • Organize recipes into folders
  • Save a recipe from a link
  • Add friends with their friend codes to see their dietary needs at a glance
  • Get warnings when a recipe conflicts with someone's dietary needs
  • See an overview of all your friends' dietary needs in one place
  • Block connections you no longer want to share with
