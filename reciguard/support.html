Frequently Asked Questions

What does ReciGuard do?

ReciGuard is a recipe manager that helps users remember their friends' dietary needs and account for them when selecting a recipe. You can create profiles to keep track of different people's dietary needs as well as connect with friends so they can fill out their own information.

How do I add a friend?

To share your profile with someone else, go to your profile and copy or share your friend code. To add a friend's profile to your friends list, go to the Friends tab and either create a manual entry or enter your friend's code. Once you're connected, you can filter recipes based on their dietary needs.

How do I track dietary needs for myself and others?

When editing either your own profile or a friend's profile, there is the option to edit dietary needs. This opens a checklist of common dietary restrictions as well as the option to enter your own. Each entry can also have a severity level set for it. You can find these profile under the Friends tab. This information will be saved to the appropriate folder and can then be viewed in the Dietary Needs Overview or used to filter recipes and see relevant warnings.

What happens if I remove a friend, or they delete their account?

Removing a friend stops sharing your information with them and unlinks your profile from their personal friend entry immediately. If a friend deletes their account or stops sharing, their name and any private notes you wrote about them stay in your list as a snapshot while the information they entered is no longer available. You can remove that entry at any time.

How do I block someone?

You can block another user by clicking the link icon on the Friends tab (Sharing With). From here, you can block anyone you are already sharing with. If you want to block a user you are not currently sharing with, you can click the Blocked page from here and then select the option to Block by Friend Code. Blocking another user will stop sharing with them, unlink them from any friend profiles you have linked to them, and prevent them from reconnecting with you.

How do I add a recipe?

Click the + button in the center of the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen and then select an input method to add a new recipe.

Is my data synced across devices?

Yes. Your recipes, folders, and friends are stored securely with our backend provider and are available whenever you sign in.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No. ReciGuard has no ads and never sells or shares your data with third-party advertisers. See the Privacy Policy for more details.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Settings tab in the app and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, recipes, folders, and dietary information and cannot be undone.

Is ReciGuard available on Android?

ReciGuard is currently only available for iOS. Let us know if you would like to see an Android version and we will consider it for future development.
